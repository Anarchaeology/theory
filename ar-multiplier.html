What the readings show

  • The Doctor is above the Bureaucrat, not below. The old numbers give Doctors the lowest figure of all and put the Bureaucrat above them. In game the Doctor's multiplier is above the Bureaucrat's at every AR past 1000, and by AR 2,500 it is above the Trader's too. This was checked with one weapon on both: at about AR 2,075 the Doctor's crit minus normal hit read 2,772 and the Bureaucrat's 2,661, where the old numbers predict 2,649 and 2,707.
  • The Keeper and the Fixer climb in stairs, steep stretches between flatter ones. Their old numbers, 0.34 and 0.30, are about their averages. But past about AR 2,450 the next bit of AR is worth about 0.2 per 400 AR for both, well under either.
  • The Doctor climbs far faster than its old number says: about 0.18 per 400 AR on average, against 0.10. At AR 2,599 it is 8% above its old line.
  • The Trader stays on its old line, within 0.7% of it the whole way.
  • The Bureaucrat climbs in two straight stretches with a knee near AR 1,350: 0.16 per 400 AR up to it and 0.08 after. At AR 2,500 it is 2.9% under its old line.

About these numbers

The multiplier. It multiplies the weapon's damage roll and its crit bonus. Add damage is outside it. Up to AR 1000 it is AR/400 + 1 for every profession, which makes it 3.5 at AR 1000. AR is weapon skill plus add-all-offense, and AR past the weapon's max beneficial skill doesn't count.

The old numbers. These are the figures in common use before these readings: one number per profession for how much the multiplier gains per 400 AR past AR 1000. The Keeper's 0.34 means 400 more AR adds 0.34, where below AR 1000 it would add a full 1. That makes a straight line for each profession, drawn dashed on the chart.

The readings. On a target with enough armor to hold every normal hit to its minimum, a crit minus a normal hit is the weapon's crit bonus times the multiplier, because crits don't roll. So one crit and one normal hit at an AR give the multiplier there. The only error is the game rounding damage to whole points. Each profession was read from about AR 1000 up to the AR shown in the table.

The data. The curves on this page are in one file: ar-multiplier.json.
