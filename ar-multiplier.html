About these numbers

The multiplier. It multiplies the weapon's damage roll and its crit bonus. Add damage is outside it. Up to AR 1000 it is AR/400 + 1 for every profession, which makes it 3.5 at AR 1000. AR is weapon skill plus add-all-offense, and AR past the weapon's max beneficial skill doesn't count.

The old numbers. These are the figures in common use before these readings: one number per profession for how much the multiplier gains per 400 AR past AR 1000. The Keeper's 0.34 means 400 more AR adds 0.34, where below AR 1000 it would add a full 1. That makes a straight line for each profession, drawn dashed on the chart.

The readings. On a target with enough armor to hold every normal hit to its minimum, a crit minus a normal hit is the weapon's crit bonus times the multiplier, because crits don't roll. So one crit and one normal hit at an AR give the multiplier there. The only error is the game rounding damage to whole points. Each profession was read from about AR 1000 up to the AR shown in the table.

The data. The curves on this page are in one file: ar-multiplier.json.
