By the hit formula

The crit chance by the measured formula at each Critical Increase. Up to a hit formula of 100%, it stays at 3% + Critical Increase. Past 100%, every 6 points of hit formula over 100% add 1 point of crit chance. For example, a hit formula of 130% (approximately 4,500 AR against The Night Heart's 1,800 defense) adds 5 points to your crit chance.

About these numbers

The measured formula. Crit chance is the share of landed hits that crit. The last part counts only past 100%. crit chance = 3% + Critical Increase + (hit formula − 100%) ÷ 6

The hit formula. The hit chance before it's held at 100%. Past 100% every swing lands, but the formula keeps counting. The slider is the defender's, from −100 at full defensive to +100 at full aggressive. hit formula = 50% + (AR − Def) ÷ (Def + |AR − Def|) + (slider + 100) ÷ 500

Critical Increase. The attacker's Critical Increase, as items, perks, and buffs list it.

The results. Two players swung at each other at a range of AR, defense and the defender's slider, and the crits among the hits that landed were counted.

The data. The results on this page are in one file: crit.json.
