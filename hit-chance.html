About these numbers

The measured formula. The slider is the defender's, from −100 at full defensive to +100 at full aggressive. hit chance = 50% + (AR − Def) ÷ (Def + |AR − Def|) + (slider + 100) ÷ 500

AR and defense. AR is weapon skill plus add-all-offense. The weapon's max beneficial skill doesn't cap it here, as it does for damage. Defense is the evade skill the attack checks plus add-all-defense.

The linear formula. The one in common use. hit chance = AR ÷ (√2 × Def) + slider ÷ 500

The results. Two players swung at each other with AR from 0.13 to 2 times the defense, at three of the defender's slider settings. The measured formula has no fitted numbers in it. Half the results are within 0.8 points of it and none is more than 2.5 off. That is a little more scatter than chance alone gives: one of the two characters landed about 0.7 points more than the other at the same AR, defense and slider, and nothing measured so far explains it.

Other observations. Only the ratio of AR to defense matters, not their raw values: the results held at defenses from 238 to 1,520. The attacker's own slider doesn't matter. Add-all counts the same as skill, for AR and for defense. Crit chance doesn't change the hit chance.

The data. The results on this page are in one file: hit-chance.json.
