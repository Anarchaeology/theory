About these numbers

The measured formula. The hit chance formula with nano skill for AR and nano resist for defense, and a flat 4.2 points added. The slider is the target's, from −100 at full defensive to +100 at full aggressive. The caster's slider has no effect. land chance = 50% + (NS − NR) ÷ (NR + |NS − NR|) + (slider + 100) ÷ 500 + 4.2%

Nano skill and nano resist. Nano skill is the caster's skill in what the nano casts on, and nano resist the target's. Add-all-offense and add-all-defense don't count. The results were taken with Corruption, which casts on Matter Creation and Time and Space at 50% each against Nano Resist, with the two skills kept equal.

The results. Two players cast a nano at each other with nano skill from 0.028 to 2.47 times the nano resist, at three of the target's slider settings.

Other observations. Only the ratio of nano skill to nano resist matters, not their raw values: the results held with nano skills from 36 to 1,300. Only the target's slider matters, and it adds 20 points for every 100, as it does for a swing. Even at 0.028 times the nano resist about 1 cast in 20 landed, and from about 1.85 times it every cast did.

The data. The results on this page are in one file: nano-chance.json.
